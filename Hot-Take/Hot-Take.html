Was Juice WRLD the Greatest Freestyle Rapper?

Introduction

Juice WRLD was an American rapper, songwriter, and singer famous for his hit "Lucid Dreams."

This is a picture of Juice WRLD:


Many consider Juice WRLD as one of the best freestylers too.


Why?

Juice WRLD had the ability to freestyle for minutes without stopping, showing amazing creativity and emotion.

His freestyles were often emotional and complex, which set him apart from many others.


Disagreement

However, some argue other rappers like Eminem or Tory Lanez are better freestylers.

Freestyling is subjective, and different fans have different favorites.
